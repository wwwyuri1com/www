**Full Name:** DDD Golden **Chinese Name:** 金 --- **IP:** DDD **Primary Appearances:** DDD Presents **Character Traits:** Straightforward, airheaded, serious, protective, impressively well-endowed, submissive type **Hidden Traits:** Frighteningly serious when she gets serious, tsundere, occasionally unwilling to admit her feelings **Primary Love Interest:** E.Red, N.White **Catchphrase / Mannerism:** "Hey!" Her striking figure tends to draw attention, but she is actually a very straightforward girl. Contrary to the impression her appearance gives, she is not good with flirtatious or intimate topics and tends toward pure romance. She frequently plays roles such as bodyguards and security personnel.
