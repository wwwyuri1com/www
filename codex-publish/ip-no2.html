**Full Name:** NO2 Ink **Chinese Name:** NO2 --- **IP:** NO1 **Primary Appearances:** NO1 / IP Collab **Character Traits:** Playfully cynical, clever, humorous, languid, composed, highly observant, elusive, exceptionally intelligent, highly skilled in combat, adept at manipulating situations, rarely disturbed by danger **Hidden Traits:** Cheat-level intelligence, Final Boss Attributes, sovereign presence, enjoys watching events unfold, quietly protects those she accepts, easily loses interest in weak or uninteresting opponents, occasionally displays an unexpectedly gentle kindness **Primary Love Interest:** N.Wind (NO3) **Catchphrase / Mannerism:** Soft laughter Yawning Watching situations unfold with an amused smile Becoming even more composed as danger increases Often treating events as games, chess matches, or interesting developments --- **Character Introduction:** One of the core members of the NO1 Office and a long-time actress appearing throughout its various script series. N.Ink possesses an almost unfair level of comprehension. She often understands the rules before the people inside a story do, sees through situations long before they fully unfold, and sometimes seems aware that possibilities exist beyond the script itself. As a result, very little genuinely unsettles her. More often, she simply stands to the side with an amused smile, watching events develop. Like watching a show. Or playing a game of chess. Her playful cynicism does not mean she is indifferent. Across many scripts, she displays a strange but consistent form of kindness. Even if everything is ultimately nothing more than a dream, she seems to believe it should at least be a dream worth seeing through to the end. She can manipulate a situation just as easily as she can bring one to an end. Within the NO1 Office, the relationship between N.Ink (NO2) and N.Wind (NO3) is particularly distinctive. One resembles the ink at the farthest edge of midnight. The other resembles a wind that never stops moving.
