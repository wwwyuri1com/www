**Version Selection** You can switch between different versions of the work using the version controls: **AI (EN)** provides the primary English reading experience and is the recommended version for most readers. **Human Draft (Traditional Chinese)** presents the original prompt-based story script with instruction-related content removed. Readers who can read Traditional Chinese are also welcome to experience this version. **Human Prompt (Traditional Chinese)** presents the original prompt, including its instruction-related content. This version is intended for readers who are interested in seeing the source material and AI generation process. **A + H + P** displays all available versions together when no individual version is selected. ※ Published works still receive final human editing and may therefore differ slightly from the original source material. --- **Upper-Left Controls** **Dark Mode** allows you to switch the article text area between a light and dark reading appearance for greater reading comfort. *Only applies to article text.* It affects only the article text area, not the rest of the website. **Reading Font** allows you to adjust the font, font size, and line spacing used for English and Chinese article text. --- **Lower Controls** **Favorite** adds or removes the current article from your favorites. Favorited articles are displayed at the top of the Codex list. **Hidden Post** hides the current article from the Codex list. Hidden articles can still be found under **Codex > Show Hidden**. To unhide an article, open the article and click this button again. **Clear Reading Progress** removes the saved reading progress for the current article. **Save Reading Progress** updates the saved reading position for the current article. Please note that reading progress is saved separately for each version: **AI, Human Draft, Human Prompt, and A + H + P.** **Read Done** adds or removes the Read Done status for the current article. Completed articles can be easily identified for later review and will also display a special marker in the Codex list. **Immersion Mode** hides the interface and provides a more spacious and comfortable reading area.
