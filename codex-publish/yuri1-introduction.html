Full Text · YURI NO1

**YURI NO1** YURI NO1 (also referred to as “YURI1”) is a mixed-media entertainment IP created in 2026, focusing on Girls' Love (YURI) creation through original characters, fictional worlds, stories, novels, and other creative works. YURI1 presents its works through a dual Human Creation × AI Expansion experience. The website currently provides the English AI-expanded version as the primary reading experience, while the original Human Draft and Human Prompt are preserved in Traditional Chinese as part of the creative process presented with each work. YURI1’s identity lies not only in the works themselves, but also in how those works are created and presented. AI-expanded English novels, AI-generated illustrations, and selected original manuscripts and prompts are all part of the YURI1 experience. Readers may explore not only the completed work, but also different stages of its creation, from human concepts and original scripts to AI-assisted expansion and visual generation. Rather than treating AI as a completely hidden production tool, YURI1 presents the AI-assisted creative process itself as part of the experience. **IP Works** IP Works are series built around a recurring core group of main characters. Different works may follow the same group of primary characters while exploring different stories, themes, and creative formats. **Non-IP Works** Non-IP Works are independent stories that do not revolve around a fixed core group of characters. Each work may feature different characters and is primarily designed as a self-contained story. Characters may also make cameo appearances or reappear across different works when appropriate. YURI1 does not require a fixed chronological reading order. There is no required timeline connecting individual works or chapters, and readers do not need to follow a specific sequence. You may freely choose any work or chapter that interests you. **Features of AI Generation Use** AI is used throughout the creative process of YURI1 as an assisting tool for transforming human concepts, scripts, and creative ideas into AI-expanded text and AI-generated visual content, while also serving as part of the experimental and entertainment experience. YURI1 places greater emphasis on completing the core concept, bringing new ideas to life, and exploring the creative process itself than on making every AI-generated result absolutely reasonable, complete, or perfect. As a result, some AI-generated content may contain generation anomalies, visual imperfections, textual errors, semantic deviations, or other unexpected results. YURI1 does not treat repeated refinement of every generated result into a completely polished state as its primary goal. Once a work is able to adequately express its original concept, YURI1 may choose to retain certain traces or imperfections of the generation process and direct its creative energy toward the next new idea. This does not mean that YURI1 does not value quality. Rather, it reflects a preference to use limited creative time and resources to explore more new characters, stories, concepts, and possibilities. In other words, YURI1 does not require every generated result to be perfect. **The goal is to bring more new ideas to life.** For further information about AI use and the creative process, please refer to the related articles under **Policy**.
