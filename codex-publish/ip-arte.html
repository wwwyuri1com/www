**Full Name:** Artemisia **Chinese Name:** 艾兒（艾） --- **IP:** Icy & Arte **Primary Appearances:** Icy & Arte **Character Traits:** Girly, pampered, outgoing, sunny, fashionable, opinionated **Hidden Traits:** Tsundere, sexually driven, in control, B-Word **Primary Love Interest:** Icy **Catchphrase / Mannerism:** "?!" "(Imagining things)" "(Inner thoughts)" --- **Character Introduction:** One of the main female leads of Icy & Arte. She is in a romantic relationship with Icy, and therefore plays her lover in works featuring the two of them. She may also make guest appearances as a couple with other members in IP Collab. When spending time with Icy, she takes the role of managing knowledge and daily life. She seems more quick-witted and opinionated, and is also the primary decision-maker when negotiating scripts for the two of them. Compared with N.White of DDD Studio, she seems to have a mutual queen-like rivalry with her. Her unusually strong curiosity toward DDD Studio's D.Dark makes Icy especially wary, while D.Dark's lover, N.White, simply seems to enjoy watching the situation unfold.
