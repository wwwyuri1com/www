**Full Name:** NO5 Wing **Chinese Name:** NO5 --- **IP:** NO1 **Primary Appearances:** NO1 / IP Collab **Character Traits:** Sweet, curious, playful, cheerful, elegant, graceful, lightly mischievous, highly perceptive, affectionate, energetic, naturally inquisitive, exceptionally powerful, high-level combat capability **Hidden Traits:** Top-tier angelic combat power, unexpectedly overwhelming strength, loves observing interesting situations, enjoys playful experiments, easily distracted by cute or amusing ideas, notices romantic tension quickly, more perceptive than her carefree behavior suggests, capable of becoming extremely serious when necessary **Primary Love Interest:** Unconfirmed **Catchphrase / Mannerism:** Tilting her head when curious Soft laughter Knocking before entering — sometimes Showing up with snacks or small gifts Suddenly thinking of unrelated but amusing ideas Watching people closely while pretending she is only casually curious “Just curious.” “Don’t underestimate me, okay?” --- **Character Introduction:** One of the core members of the NO1 Office and a long-time actress appearing throughout its various script series. N.Wing is cheerful, elegant, highly curious, and almost impossible to keep away from anything she finds interesting. She frequently approaches situations with a soft smile, a tilted head, and the sincere belief that asking one more question could only make things more fun. Across different scripts, she has portrayed mysterious high-level beings, angels, hidden observers, guides, shapeshifters, and entities capable of quietly influencing entire events from behind the scenes. This often creates a peculiar contrast. Her curiosity is not superficial. N.Wing pays close attention to people. She notices emotional changes, relationships, unusual behavior, and small details others may overlook. However, unlike N.Wind, who tends to observe with controlled distance, N.Wing usually wants to move closer, ask questions, test a reaction, or quietly participate herself. This tendency has occasionally made others hesitant to include her in highly serious meetings. Not because she lacks ability. Quite the opposite. The problem is that no one can predict where her thoughts will go once the discussion begins.
