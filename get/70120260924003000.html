Lesna was the kind of woman who made success look effortless.

In her early thirties, she was already the founder and CEO of a technology company that had gone from an obscure research startup to an industry darling almost overnight, thanks to a generous round of private investment.

She was intelligent, composed, meticulous, and remarkably difficult to persuade once she'd made up her mind.

She was also beautiful.

Unfortunately, none of those qualities had helped her find a girlfriend.

For all her professional achievements, Lesna's romantic experience amounted to a collection of disappointing conversations, awkward dates, and several perfectly pleasant evenings that had somehow ended without a second invitation.

She simply couldn't understand it.

Wasn't romance supposed to begin with mutual understanding? Shared values? Perhaps an appreciation for literature?

Apparently not.

Still, Lesna refused to give up.

Every evening after work, she opened a dating platform for women, logged into her anonymous account, and updated her little corner of the internet.

Lady L

*Occupation: Office Worker*

She had deliberately concealed her position. The last thing she wanted was someone pursuing her for her money.

Her latest diary entry read:

*If you were the wind, I would follow wherever you wandered.*

*If you were the sea, I would wait upon your shore.*

*Somewhere beneath the same sky, perhaps you are waiting for me, too.*

Lesna reviewed her writing.

"Beautiful."

She pressed Publish.

The post received three likes.

One of them was from an account advertising cryptocurrency.

Lesna sighed.

Perhaps true love required patience.

A smiling woman with a carefree expression had one arm draped around an enormous leopard.

An actual leopard.

"My goodness."

Her interests included music, socializing, outdoor adventures, and meeting interesting women.
