Leanna had a reputation.

At work, she was the woman who could silence an entire conference room simply by putting down her pen.

Her suits were immaculate, her decisions final, and her patience for incompetence practically nonexistent.

"Why is this report still using last quarter's figures?"

Nobody answered.

"I asked a question."

The department manager quickly opened her laptop.

Leanna watched in silence.

"Correct it. I want the revised version before three."

"Yes, ma'am!"

And that was that.

Another perfectly ordinary afternoon for one of the company's most intimidating executives.

What nobody knew was that Leanna had a secret.

A very small, very pink secret.

On a dating app for women, she went by the name Little Bunny.

And Little Bunny was nothing like Leanna.

Little Bunny: Hiiii~! Is anyone free to keep Bunny company tonight? 🐰💕

Little Bunny: Bunny's had such a looong day~ Someone give her a huggg! ♡

Even her voice changed.

The woman who could deliver a devastating performance review without blinking would suddenly sound as though she'd been dipped in honey.

"Awww~ You're so sweet! Little Bunny's getting shy nowww~"

Sometimes she practiced the voice while getting ready for bed.

Sometimes she practiced it in front of the mirror.

And sometimes—

Her work phone rang.

"Yes?"

Instantly, the sweetness vanished.

"No. That's unacceptable. Reschedule the meeting and send me the corrected documents."

Click.

She returned to her dating app.

"Hehe~ Bunny's back! Did you miss her? 🐰💕"

It was a remarkable transformation.

And one she intended to keep strictly confidential.

Finding a girlfriend in real life shouldn't have been difficult.

Leanna was attractive, financially independent, and successful.

Unfortunately, her company's culture was conservative, and she had absolutely no intention of letting an office romance ruin her carefully maintained reputation.

Besides, there was another problem.

She attracted admirers.

Lots of them.

The sort of women who admired her confidence, her authority, her ability to take charge.

That was flattering, certainly.

But privately, Leanna wanted something very different.

She wanted someone elegant and dependable.

Someone whose shoulder she could lean against after a long day.

Someone who would let her be Little Bunny without laughing.

One of her colleagues had recently hinted at having romantic feelings for her.

A perfectly lovely woman.

Unfortunately, she was also the extremely sweet, clingy type.

Leanna had briefly imagined what dating her might look like.

"Bunny wants a huggg~!"

"Awww, me toooo~!"

"Then come hold Bunny!"

"Nooo, you hold me first!"

"But Bunny wants to be spoiled!"

"So do I!"

Two grown women stood facing each other, arms outstretched, both waiting for the other to make the first move.

Neither moved.

Eventually, imaginary Leanna began negotiating cuddle arrangements as if chairing a board meeting.

She immediately dismissed the entire scenario.

'Absolutely not.'

She wanted to be the one receiving affection.

Just once.

Was that really so unreasonable?

One evening, Little Bunny received a new follower.

Wild Lilac

Leanna tapped the profile.

Then stopped breathing for approximately two seconds.

The woman in the photographs was tall, graceful, and strikingly beautiful.

Long hair framed a composed face, and her cool expression suggested someone who rarely wasted words.

There was something intellectual about her.

Something distant.

Something almost impossible to approach.

'Oh.'

Leanna stared at the photograph.

'That's her.'

Not literally, of course.

But this was precisely the kind of woman Little Bunny had always dreamed of meeting.

She typed a greeting.

Deleted it.

Typed another.

Deleted that too.

Finally—

Little Bunny: H-hello~! Bunny's happy to meet you! 🐰💕

Wild Lilac: Hello. Nice to meet you too.

No hearts.

No excessive punctuation.

Not even a single bunny.

Leanna pressed a hand to her chest.

'She's perfect.'

They chatted for a while before moving to a voice call.

"Hello, Little Bunny."

Leanna nearly dropped her phone.

Wild Lilac's voice was calm, measured, and slightly low.

Every word sounded effortless.

"H-hiiii~!"

Leanna's own voice immediately became twice as sweet.

"Little Bunny's so happy to hear your voice~"

"That's nice to hear."

Just that.

A simple, ordinary reply.

Yet Leanna was already imagining herself sitting beside this woman, leaning against her shoulder while being gently stroked on the head.

'Poor Little Bunny. You've worked so hard today. Come here.'

'Yes, please...'

"Little Bunny?"

"Hm? Y-yes~?"

"May I ask you something?"

"Of courseee~!"

A pause.

"Do you happen to work at **** Company?"

Leanna froze.

Her imaginary romantic scene shattered.

"...Huh?"

"It's just that you sound a little like someone I've seen around the business park."

'What?'

Leanna stared at her phone.

'Who is this woman?'

She searched her memory.

Clients? Executives? Business partners?

Nothing.

She would certainly have remembered someone this beautiful.

Wild Lilac continued.

"Although the woman I'm thinking of is very different from you."

Leanna swallowed.

"Ohhh~? Is she cute like Little Bunny? 🐰"

"Not exactly."

"Eh?"

"She's very serious. Quite intimidating, actually."

'Oh, no.'

"And she seems rather difficult to approach."

'OH, NO.'

Leanna's mind immediately assembled an emergency response committee.

Risk assessment.

Identity exposure.

Reputational damage.

Emergency evacuation.

Possible relocation to another continent.

"Little Bunny? Are you still there?"

"Y-yesss~! Bunny's right here! 🐰💕"

She had never sounded less convincing in her life.

Leanna tried to investigate without sounding suspicious.

"Sooo~ how do you know this mysterious woman?"

"I don't really know her."

"Really?"

"I pass through that business park quite often."

Wild Lilac sounded almost amused.

"I've seen her walking into the same company several times."

'Wonderful. Excellent. I'm finished.'

"And once or twice, I've heard her speaking rather loudly on the phone."

Leanna closed her eyes.

She remembered a particularly disastrous afternoon involving a server outage and three departments arguing over responsibility.

She had not been especially gentle that day.

'Was that the day?'

"The woman I saw was scolding someone so fiercely that I could hear her from across the pavement."

'It was definitely that day.'

"But I thought she was impressive."

Leanna blinked.

"...Impressive?"

"Confident. Strong-willed. Someone who clearly knows what she's doing."

For the first time, Leanna didn't know how to respond.

Then Wild Lilac added, quietly:

"I've actually wanted to talk to her for a while."

Leanna's heart gave an unexpected little jump.

"But I never found the right opportunity."

"Ohhh~"

Little Bunny's voice returned almost by instinct.

"Does Wild Lilac have a crush on that scary woman? 🐰💕"

"Perhaps."

Leanna sat perfectly still.

"Especially if she turns out to be someone as sweet as Little Bunny after work."

Silence.

Leanna looked at her phone.

Then at the wall.

Then back at her phone.

'Excuse me?'

Her imagination immediately began constructing another scene.

Wild Lilac stood beside her, cool and elegant, while Leanna rested against her shoulder.

"You're safe with me, Little Bunny."

'Ahhh...'

"And I like that serious side of you, too."

'AHHH...'

"Little Bunny?"

Leanna snapped upright.

"Y-yes!"

"Are you all right?"

"Perfectly fineee~! 🐰💕"

She was not perfectly fine.

She was experiencing a complete executive-level system failure.

The following morning, Leanna walked through the business park looking at every passing car.

A white sedan.

A black SUV.

A silver hatchback.

Could that be her?

What about that one?

"Director?"

Leanna nearly jumped.

A colleague stood nearby.

"Are you looking for someone?"

"No."

Her voice returned to its familiar icy tone.

"Go clock in."

"Yes, ma'am."

The colleague hurried away.

Leanna adjusted her collar.

'Compose yourself.'

She glanced at the road one final time.

'...But which car was hers?'

That evening, Wild Lilac called again.

Leanna accepted so quickly that she nearly knocked over her tea.

"Hiiii~!"

"Hello, Little Bunny."

There it was.

That voice.

Leanna smiled helplessly.

Wild Lilac hesitated.

"I hope this won't make you uncomfortable, but I think I might have been right yesterday."

Leanna stopped smiling.

"Ohhh?"

"I saw that woman again this morning."

A pause.

"She seemed to be looking for someone."

'...'

'She saw me.'

Leanna covered her face.

Wild Lilac spoke more softly.

"I'm sorry. Perhaps I'm assuming too much."

That made Leanna pause.

There was no teasing in her voice.

No accusation.

Only a gentle attempt to give her room to answer.

"Can I tell you something?" Wild Lilac asked.

"Mm-hmm~"

"I like the way you speak to me. You sound happy and playful."

Leanna held her breath.

"But I also like that other woman. The serious one."

"Even if she's scary?"

"Even then."

"What if... Little Bunny isn't always cute?"

"I wouldn't expect anyone to be."

"What if she's bossy?"

A quiet laugh.

"I think I could manage."

"And what if she gets angry at people?"

"Then I suppose I'd have to find out why."

Leanna stared at the screen.

For years, she had treated her two personalities as entirely separate worlds.

The capable executive everyone respected.

And Little Bunny, who wanted nothing more than to be held and adored.

Yet this woman was telling her that neither side needed to disappear.

"Would you think she's pretending?" Leanna asked quietly.

"Why would I?"

Wild Lilac seemed genuinely puzzled.

"People behave differently when they're working. That doesn't make the rest of them any less real."

Leanna lowered her eyes.

For once, she forgot to use her sweet voice.

"...Thank you."

There was a brief silence.

Then Wild Lilac answered.

"You're welcome."

She hadn't mentioned the change in Leanna's voice.

And somehow, that small kindness made Leanna like her even more.

"Would you like to meet for coffee this weekend?"

The invitation came so naturally that Leanna almost missed it.

"Yesss~! Of course! 🐰💕"

She froze.

'Too fast!'

"Wonderful," Wild Lilac replied. "Saturday afternoon?"

"Saturday sounds lovelyyy~"

'Stop talking like that!'

"Then it's a date."

Leanna sat motionless.

'A date.'

'A DATE.'

After the call, she spent a full hour deciding what to wear.

A business suit would be too severe.

A frilly pink dress might be too much.

Eventually, she settled on something soft and elegant, with just enough sweetness to make Little Bunny feel comfortable.

She studied herself in the mirror.

"Hello~ I'm Little Bunny! 🐰💕"

Absolutely not.

She tried again.

"Hello. It's a pleasure to meet you."

That sounded like she was interviewing a job applicant.

Leanna groaned.

'How does anyone manage a normal first date?'

Saturday arrived.

The café was tucked along a quiet street, with tall windows and small flower arrangements on each table.

Leanna spotted Wild Lilac almost immediately.

She was even more beautiful in person.

Long hair fell neatly over her shoulders, and her calm expression softened slightly when their eyes met.

The woman stood.

"Little Bunny?"

Leanna's heart nearly stopped.

"...Hello."

Her voice sounded unusually small.

Wild Lilac smiled.

"I'm glad you came."

Leanna nodded.

They sat down.

"Would you like coffee?"

Another nod.

"Or tea?"

A tiny shake of her head.

"Something sweet?"

A pause.

"...Maybe."

Wild Lilac's smile widened.

"All right."

Leanna stared at the table.

'Congratulations.'

'You can manage an entire corporation, but apparently you can't order a drink.'

Fortunately, Wild Lilac seemed in no hurry.

They talked about ordinary things.

The weather.

Books.

The café's surprisingly complicated dessert menu.

Eventually, Leanna began to relax.

When their drinks arrived, Wild Lilac looked at her over the rim of her cup.

"May I ask your name?"

Leanna hesitated.

Then she smiled.

"Leanna."

"Christine."

Wild Lilac offered her hand across the table.

"It's lovely to meet you properly, Leanna."

Leanna took it.

For a moment, neither woman spoke.

Then Leanna looked down at their joined hands.

"Christine..."

"Yes?"

"I'd appreciate it if you kept my private life confidential."

"Of course."

The answer came without hesitation.

"I wouldn't share something personal about you without your permission."

Leanna breathed out.

"Thank you."

Christine gently squeezed her hand.

"And for what it's worth, I still like Little Bunny."

Leanna's cheeks warmed.

"Even after meeting the terrifying business-park witch?"

Christine laughed softly.

"Especially after meeting her."

Leanna looked up.

"You're serious?"

"Completely."

A small smile touched Christine's lips.

"Although I have to admit, I was surprised."

"By what?"

"She's much quieter over coffee than she is on the phone."

Leanna covered her face.

"Christine!"

There it was.

Her real voice.

Firm, clear, unmistakably Leanna.

Christine smiled.

"See? I like that voice too."

Leanna lowered her hands.

For several seconds, she simply looked at the woman sitting across from her.

Then her expression softened.

"...Can Little Bunny ask you something?"

"Certainly."

"Would it be all right if we met again?"

Christine glanced at their hands, still resting together on the table.

"I was hoping you'd ask."

Leanna smiled.

Not the carefully practiced expression she wore at work.

Not the exaggerated sweetness she'd perfected online.

Just a genuine, rather helpless smile.

"Then..."

She leaned forward slightly.

"Little Bunny would really, really like that~ 🐰💕"

Christine laughed.

Outside, the afternoon sunlight spilled across the street.

Inside, a formidable corporate executive sat holding hands with the woman she'd been afraid to meet.

Her coffee was getting cold.

Her phone had been silent for nearly an hour.

And for the first time in a very long while, Leanna wasn't worried about either of those things.

Little Bunny had finally found someone who wanted to meet both of her.

And this time—

She didn't have to choose.
