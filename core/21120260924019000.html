**Character Introduction:**

The third member of DDD Studio. Her eye-catching figure naturally draws attention, but she is actually a very straightforward girl.

Despite her confident appearance, she is easily flustered when it comes to romance and prefers simple, sincere relationships. Even as she gradually grows closer to someone she is interested in, she rarely takes the initiative and usually lets the other person take the lead.

Thanks to her physical training, she frequently takes on roles such as bodyguards and security personnel.
