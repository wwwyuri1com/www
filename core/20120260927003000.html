**Character Introduction:**

Originally hired as a short-term actress for the DDD Studio short story *Sorry*, C.Curo was recruited by N.White after earning her admiration, becoming the sixth member to join the studio after L.Night.

She becomes another one of DDD Studio's cool, masculine-leaning members, with little emphasis on traditionally feminine charm.

The studio members jokingly refer to her as "an alternate version of L.Night", which creates a subtle sense of competition between her and L.Night.

However, C.Curo herself has no hostility toward L.Night. When L.Night deliberately provokes her, she simply refuses to back down and will calmly throw the remarks back at her.

This unexpectedly gives D.Golden an ally, due to the subtle competitive tension between D.Golden and L.Night stemming from their respective romantic interests.

C.Curo tends to speak in very short sentences, though it is unclear whether this is simply her personality or whether she is actually uncomfortable with social interaction.
