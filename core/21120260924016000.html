**Character Introduction:**

One of the main female leads of Icy & Arte. She is in a romantic relationship with Icy, and therefore plays her lover in works featuring the two of them.

She may also make guest appearances as a couple with other members in IP Collab.

When spending time with Icy, she takes the role of managing knowledge and daily life. She seems more quick-witted and opinionated, and is also the primary decision-maker when negotiating scripts for the two of them.

Compared with N.White of DDD Studio, she seems to have a mutual queen-like rivalry with her.

Her unusually strong curiosity toward DDD Studio's D.Dark makes Icy especially wary, while D.Dark's lover, N.White, simply seems to enjoy watching the situation unfold.

Lately, her love life seems to be facing a crisis involving a third party. Or perhaps she doesn't mind it all that much...?
