**Character Introduction:**

The third member of DDD Studio. Her striking figure tends to draw attention, but she is actually a very straightforward girl.

Contrary to the impression her appearance gives, she is not good with flirtatious or intimate topics and tends toward pure romance. Even when she gradually grows closer to someone she is interested in, she rarely takes the initiative to push the relationship toward greater intimacy.

She frequently plays roles such as bodyguards and security personnel.
