YURI1's main websites (www, music, oc, and social) now support installation as Progressive Web Apps (PWAs). In particular, music.yuri1.com offers a fullscreen, immersive experience on mobile devices.

We are also aware of an issue where music playback may stop automatically when a device becomes idle or enters sleep mode. Unfortunately, we have not yet found a reliable solution to prevent this behavior.

Installing the PWA is optional and is not the default way to browse our websites. If preferred, you can set the app to "Don't open supported links" to prevent the PWA from taking over links opened in your browser.
