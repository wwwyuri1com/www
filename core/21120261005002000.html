**Character Introduction:**
One of the original script concept owners of *Yuri Civilization Preservation Project*, published through DDD Studio. In addition, it has been determined that there is another individual known as NO1, who serves as the lead figure behind the scripts.

NO6 is suspected to be one of the parallel versions of NO1 and has named herself NO6 for ease of identification.  
Like NO1, she occasionally speaks with a strange dialect or accent of unknown origin.

Among all known NO1 individuals, she is the most emotionally detached and taciturn. However, much like Icy, NO1, and even White of DDD, she possesses a mysterious and difficult-to-understand way of thinking.

Due to her highly mechanical responses and behavior, she is often cast as a “system” or as non-human entities.

Her earliest design is believed to date back to around 2000. She later emerged as another version of herself in the form of Icy, who became a NO1 individual in the YURI1 IP universe.

However, something appears to have gone wrong during the “transfer” that resulted in Icy, causing an unexpected shift in personality and creating the uniquely approachable and down-to-earth Icy known today.

Ironically, it was this very error that caught the attention and interest of NO6, the original version herself.

Within works belonging to the *NO1* IP, she seems particularly interested in Icy, who portrays NO24 and Snow Rabbit Server ⛢Icy (NO?) in the series.
