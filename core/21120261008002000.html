**Character Introduction:**

One of the core members of the NO1 Office and a long-time actress appearing throughout its various script series.

NO3 is known for her elegance, intelligence, and calm presence.

Across different scripts, she has portrayed investigators, police officers, sharpshooters, hackers, researchers, mysterious heirs, executives, and even entities that seem to exist beyond ordinary human understanding.

Although those roles belong to separate stories and disconnected settings, certain qualities remain remarkably consistent.

She smiles.

She watches.

And she usually understands more than she chooses to reveal.

NO3 possesses exceptional observational ability and a natural instinct for reading people and situations.

She rarely reacts impulsively, preferring to wait until the structure of a problem becomes clear before making her move.

Her composure can make even dangerous situations appear strangely ordinary.

In roles involving combat, she is often associated with extraordinary precision, particularly as a sharpshooter.

In investigative roles, she tends to notice inconsistencies long before others realize something is wrong.

Yet her intelligence is rarely presented aggressively.

Instead, it appears through small smiles, carefully chosen words, and the quiet impression that she already knows the answer.

Her elegance should not be mistaken for distance.

NO3 is capable of warmth, humor, and subtle teasing, although these qualities are usually restrained beneath a composed exterior.

Within the NO1 Office, her relationship with NO2 is particularly distinctive.

They often appear like two players seated across the same chessboard.

One considers every possibility.

The other already sees the next move.

And neither seems particularly interested in explaining who made the first move.
