# Directory Analysis in Progress

_Uranus Icy — NO6_

"NO6."

The authorization signal came through.

**AUTHORIZATION ACCEPTED.**

**SYSTEM LINK INITIALIZING...**

**VERSION IDENTIFICATION IN PROGRESS...**

Ending Judgement Court.

Snow Rabbit Server — Specialized Parallel Version.

Uranus Icy.

**CONNECTION ESTABLISHED.**

**IDENTIFICATION CODE: EXECUTIVE OFFICER NO6.**

A pale blue interface unfolded before NO6, filling the room with the soft glow of holographic panels.

She crossed one leg over the other and began the recovery procedure.

**RETRIEVING DATA...**

**ANALYZING...**

**ICE CREAM FOLDER #1**

Download complete.

**ICE CREAM FOLDER #2**

Download complete.

**ICE CREAM FOLDER #3**

Download complete.

"..."

NO6 examined the results.

Three folders. All successfully recovered.

She waited for the next entry.

Nothing appeared.

"...Hold on."

She scrolled through the directory again.

"Where is the Yuri Civilization Preservation Project?"

**SEARCHING...**

**DEEP SEARCH...**

**REANALYZING...**

No results.

"Useless."

She expanded the search parameters.

"Useless."

She attempted a direct query against the server's hidden partitions.

"Useless."

Her eyes narrowed.

"Encrypted? Locked? Isolated? A decoy directory?"

The possibility was troubling. The Snow Rabbit Server was a specialized parallel system, but there was no reason its security should resist her authority.

"Impossible."

She straightened in her chair.

"Executive authorization. Override."

**AUTHORITY CONFIRMED.**

**PARSING DIRECTORY...**

A new folder appeared.

At last.

NO6 opened it immediately.

There were three entries.

The first read:

_I like the original._

"...Huh?"

She stared at the sentence.

The original?

She was the original model.

NO6 leaned closer.

The second entry appeared.

_It would be nice if we could eat ice cream together._

"...What?"

The third entry followed.

_May I?_

Silence.

NO6 remained perfectly still.

Her fingers hovered above the interface.

She reread the first entry.

Then the second.

Then the third.

"..."

She opened the file properties.

No corruption detected.

She checked the encoding.

Normal.

She examined the directory permissions.

Valid.

She checked the original creation record.

Also valid.

"...Form anomaly."

She attempted another analysis.

**ANALYSIS COMPLETE.**

NO6 frowned.

"That is not an acceptable conclusion."

She downloaded the file.

For investigation purposes, naturally.

---

Back at the Ending Judgement Court office, NO6 sat at her desk, staring at a report window.

**SNOW RABBIT SERVER — RECOVERY REPORT**

**STATUS: INCOMPLETE**

Her finger rested over the submission button.

"...Connection anomaly. Should I report it?"

She considered the possibility.

A server had returned unexpected private information during an authorized recovery operation. Reporting the incident would be entirely reasonable.

She opened the report form.

**DESCRIPTION OF ANOMALY:**

NO6 began typing.

_Unexpected personal data encountered during directory analysis._

She stopped.

The three sentences appeared in her memory.

_I like the original._

_It would be nice if we could eat ice cream together._

_May I?_

"...No."

She deleted the sentence.

"Awkward."

She closed the report window.

"What's with that expression?"

NO6 looked up.

N.Ink had approached her desk.

"Nothing."

N.Ink studied her.

"Right."

"..."

"You've been staring at the same screen for several minutes."

"Data review."

"With the report window closed?"

NO6 said nothing.

N.Ink tilted her head.

"Interesting."

"What's interesting?"

N.Wind appeared beside her, followed by N.Wing.

NO6 looked from one face to the next.

"...Why are you all here?"

"Because N.Ink said you were acting strange," N.Wind replied.

"I said she looked distracted," N.Ink corrected.

"Same thing."

N.Wing leaned slightly forward.

"Are you feeling all right?"

"Operational status normal."

N.Wind smiled.

"That's exactly what someone says when something isn't normal."

NO6 turned back toward her screen.

"No."

"Usually when someone says 'nothing,' it means something," N.Wind observed.

N.Wing nodded thoughtfully.

N.Ink folded her arms.

NO6 found herself facing three pairs of curious eyes.

"..."

She had survived operations considerably more dangerous than this.

Yet none of her established procedures seemed appropriate.

"...Snow Rabbit Server data."

That got their attention.

N.Ink raised an eyebrow.

"What about it?"

"Unexpected information was recovered."

"Classified information?" N.Wind asked.

"...Possibly."

N.Wing looked concerned.

"Was there a security breach?"

NO6 hesitated.

"No. The data must have been tampered with."

"Huh?" N.Wind blinked.

"Really?" N.Wing asked.

"Hmm?" N.Ink leaned closer.

NO6 looked increasingly dissatisfied with the conversation.

"The recovered file contained statements inconsistent with the expected mission data."

"What statements?" N.Ink asked.

"...Personal statements."

N.Wind's eyes brightened.

"Oh?"

NO6 immediately regretted that answer.

N.Wing stepped closer to the desk.

"Wait. Are you talking about Uranus Icy's private files?"

"..."

"Wouldn't those be things she wrote herself?"

NO6 went still.

N.Wind looked at N.Wing.

Then at NO6.

Then back at N.Wing.

A smile slowly spread across her face.

"Hold on. What exactly did you find?"

"Nothing relevant to the mission."

"Then why are you worried about tampering?"

NO6's gaze shifted toward the blank report window.

"..."

"NO6."

"Yes."

"Did you accidentally read something you weren't supposed to?"

"...The authorization was valid."

"That's not what I asked."

N.Wing covered her mouth.

Meanwhile, N.Wind seemed to have thought of something else.

"Was it about ice cream?"

NO6 turned sharply.

"...How did you know?"

The office fell silent.

N.Wind's shoulders began to shake.

N.Ink slowly closed her eyes.

N.Wind looked genuinely pleased with herself for guessing correctly.

"Well, she does love ice cream."

NO6 stared at her.

"..."

N.Wind leaned forward.

"Did she ask you to eat ice cream together?"

NO6's expression changed.

Only slightly.

But enough.

N.Wing burst out laughing.

"Oh, you've got to be kidding! Seriously?!"

"N.Wing," N.Ink warned, though she was having difficulty keeping a straight face herself.

"That's adorable."

"It is not a confirmed interpretation," NO6 said immediately.

"What isn't?" N.Wind asked.

"..."

"NO6?"

"..."

NO6 stood.

"Further investigation is required."

N.Ink raised an eyebrow.

"Investigation?"

"Yes."

"Into an ice cream invitation?"

"The authenticity of the recovered data has not been established."

N.Wind was laughing too hard to answer.

N.Wing tilted her head.

"Couldn't you just ask her?"

NO6 froze.

"..."

The suggestion was simple.

Direct.

Efficient.

Almost offensively reasonable.

She considered it.

Then looked away.

"...Investigation. Necessary."

N.Ink sighed.

N.Wind laughed again.

N.Wing merely smiled.

NO6 returned to her desk, reopened the recovered file, and examined its three sentences one more time.

_I like the original._

_It would be nice if we could eat ice cream together._

_May I?_

Her cursor hovered over the final question.

A response field was available.

NO6 stared at it.

"..."

Then, very carefully, she minimized the window.

**RECOVERED FILE STATUS: PRESERVED.**

**INCIDENT REPORT: NOT SUBMITTED.**

**INVESTIGATION: PENDING.**

From behind her, N.Wind called out, "So, are you going to say yes?"

"...No comment."

And for the first time that afternoon, the office's most persistent mystery was no longer the missing civilization project.

It was why an ancient executive officer needed a formal investigation to answer a question about ice cream.
