If possible, I wish I could forever remember the stories shared between myself and my OCs I cherish most.

To remember their existence and their history for all eternity.

I don't believe my consciousness could truly be preserved for that long. Yet we cannot say that the future will never make it possible.

And so, it feels as though I am leaving behind a record from one of the earliest great waves of generative AI—an era when imagination first began to be brought into visible form through AI.

Perhaps a thousand years from now, or ten thousand years from now, or even in an age beyond the concept of years, like the timeless era in which NO1's Court of Final Judgment exists.

Someday, some technology may bring me back to consciousness.

I will open my eyes.

I will see them standing before me.

And I will see myself become one of them.
