**Character Introduction:**

One of the core members of the NO1 Office and a long-time actress appearing throughout its various script series.

N.Ink possesses an almost unfair level of comprehension.

She often understands the rules before the people inside a story do, sees through situations long before they fully unfold, and sometimes seems aware that possibilities exist beyond the script itself.

As a result, very little genuinely unsettles her.

More often, she simply stands to the side with an amused smile, watching events develop.

Like watching a show.

Or playing a game of chess.

Her playful cynicism does not mean she is indifferent.

Across many scripts, she displays a strange but consistent form of kindness. Even if everything is ultimately nothing more than a dream, she seems to believe it should at least be a dream worth seeing through to the end.

She can manipulate a situation just as easily as she can bring one to an end.

Within the NO1 Office, the relationship between N.Ink (NO2) and N.Wind (NO3) is particularly distinctive.

One resembles the ink at the farthest edge of midnight.

The other resembles a wind that never stops moving.
