**Character Introduction:**

The fifth member of DDD Studio, having returned from outside the studio. Due to her romantic relationship with E.Red, she co-stars with her in Loading Night, where they play the main couple.

She occasionally appears in DDD Presents, paired with other members.

She is the member of DDD Studio who favors a cool, androgynous style over emphasizing femininity, making her relatively easy to pair with other members.

Her personality is simply the way it is, so her acting range tends to be fairly consistent, often giving the impression that the atmosphere is similar in every work.

Even when asked to change, she has difficulty doing so. In the end, the team usually compromises by letting her play to her natural personality.
